## Needs and Objectives

### Primary Reasons for seeking credit

"Sam and Amy, you have advised me that you are looking to purchase your first home to provide housing security for your future and to exit the rental market. You are looking to purchase in a suburb with a good school, that is close to transport and that is in a convenient location for both of your respective careers. As you have been growing your savings, you would like a loan of no more than 80% LVR to avoid lenders mortgage insurance and purchase price of no more than $875,000. You both have stable employment and have not indicated any likely adverse changes to your current employment or financial situation and Sam, as your business is experiencing continued growth, you have indicated that an expected $10,000 per annum increase per year is likely."

### Immediate Needs and Objectives

"As you are willing to renovate a property over the next few years after you purchase, you have mentioned that flexibility of loan product is important to you should you require additional funds to complete any renovations in the short to medium term. You have requested that you are able to make your repayments weekly or fortnightly as you want to reduce interest and build equity. As your child will be starting school within the next year, you are looking to have any alterations completed prior to this time."

### Longer Term

"You also advised that you are not looking to sell this property in the medium to long term and are planning on purchasing a home that is big enough to grow your family over the next 5-10 years and will provide enough space as your child gets older. You have also indicated that you would like to pay this loan down as soon as possible, so you may own your home outright prior to retirement at age 75 with the possibility of using some of the equity in this property to purchase an investment property at a later stage."

## Executive Summary: Why Buy New in Australia? (2026)

For investors from Singapore, Hong Kong, and China, Australia represents a AAA-rated wealth sanctuary. While entry is strictly regulated — foreigners are effectively restricted to the new-build pathway from 1 April 2025 to 30 June 2029 — this legally channels capital into a chronically undersupplied market. Crucially, Australia's maximum 9% state duty surcharge provides a massive arbitrage against Singapore's 60% ABSD.

## 1. The Purchase Pathway, Compliance & Finance

- **FIRB mechanics:** approval is mandatory — **$15,600 for < $1M** (indexed 1 July 2026; 2025-26 was $15,100); ~$31,300 for $1M–$2M (2026-27 ATO schedule). Properties vacant > 183 days trigger an annual penalty **double the FIRB fee**.
- **Enforcement:** FIRB breaches carry civil penalties up to AUD 20,000 per contravention plus potential criminal prosecution for deliberate evasion. Lenders increasingly require FIRB clearance before disbursing.
- **Borrowing & remittance:** 60–70% LVR standard for non-residents; Chinese nationals on 5-year visas may reach 80% LVR; PRs accessing negative gearing up to 90%. Offshore Chinese nationals must navigate home-country USD 50,000/yr outbound remittance limits.

## 2. Exhaustive Benefit Inventory

- **Safe haven & Torrens title:** absolute title registration certainty; APRA-regulated banking.
- **Severe undersupply:** National Housing Supply Council projects a 262,000-home shortfall by 2029; migration, students and Build-to-Rent lock in low vacancy.
- **0% estate tax:** unrestricted generational wealth transfer (vs US 40% non-resident death tax).
- **Double-tax treaties (DTAs):** with Singapore and China prevent double taxation on rental income.
- **Depreciation:** new builds maximise Division 43 deductions (2.5%/yr).
- **State concessions:** **WA offers a 100% duty concession on off-the-plan purchases up to $800k** (capped $50k), valid through 30 June 2028.
- **Deal-specific developer incentives:** furniture packages, 12–24 month rental guarantees, deposit bonds, cashback credits to offset FIRB costs.

## 3. Transparent Risks, Taxes & Surcharges (2026)

- **Duty surcharges** (above standard stamp duty): NSW 9%; VIC, QLD, TAS 8%; WA, SA 7%; ACT, NT 0%.
- **Land tax surcharges:** NSW 5%, VIC 4%, QLD 3%, TAS 2%, ACT 0.75%; SA, WA, NT 0%.
- **Income tax:** non-resident rental income typically taxed in the 25–40% marginal range depending on deductions, structure (direct vs trust), offsets. Engage a qualified Australian tax advisor — outcomes vary by investor profile.
- **CGT:** foreigners lack the 50% CGT discount; 15% FRCGW applies on sale (a creditable advance, not a final flat tax).

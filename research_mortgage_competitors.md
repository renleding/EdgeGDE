## 1. Blend (blend.com)

|---|---|

## Key Findings

1. **Only the comparison sites** (Canstar, Finder, Ratehub, Moneyfacts) offer public-facing mortgage calculators.
2. **AI/agentic capabilities are concentrated** in the B2B platforms: Blend (Autopilot), ICE (Mortgage Analyzers), and TrustEngine (AI Presentation Creator).
3. **Better.com** is the only direct lender with both public calculators and an AI assistant (Betsy).
4. **Roostify** no longer operates independently — merged into Blend. **RateCity** redirects to Canstar.
5. **Geographic gaps**: No researched competitor covers all of USA, Canada, UK, and Australia natively. Blend/ICE/MortgageCoach are US-only. Comparison sites are country-specific.
6. **None of the comparison sites** offer agentic AI scoring capabilities. Their value is rate aggregation, editorial content, and calculator tools.
